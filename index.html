01

The Pricing Bottleneck

Distributor Pricing Approval → Margin Realization
01 / REQUEST
Distributor asks for off-contract pricing
Inbound via email or phone to field sales rep.
Manual lookup 02 / DATA HUNT
Rep pulls cost, comp, history across 3+ systems
CRM, pricing sheet, distributor portal. Inconsistent.
Email chain 03 / SUBMIT
Approval request drafted & emailed up
Spreadsheet attachment to pricing manager.
2–5 day lag 04 / REVIEW
Pricing manager reviews, often escalates
Finance pulled in for larger deals. Queue grows.
Margin leak 05 / DECIDE
Decision returned with little context
Approved, denied, or counter-offered. No rationale captured.
06 / OUTCOME
Order placed — or lost to competitor
No win/loss feedback into pricing model.
Directional Impact
If 3M processes ~5,000 special-price requests/month at avg $25K deal size — even 15% faster closure unlocks $18M+ in annualized at-risk revenue.
Cycle Time 2–5 days
Margin Leak 8–12% avg
Feedback Loop None

3M · Coordinated Agent Layer
System Design
01.2

Three agents. One pricing pipeline.

Read-only against existing systems → no ERP project required
Agent 01
Pricing Intelligence
TriggerNew pricing request lands in CRM or sales inbox.
InputsCustomer purchase history · margin floor rules · competitor pricing · contract terms.
ReasoningRecommend optimal price + win-probability score, bounded by margin guardrails.
OutputPre-filled approval request with rationale and risk flag.
IntegrationsCRM · pricing engine · ERP (read-only).
Human-in-loop Sales rep validates recommendation before submitting — keeps reps in the driver's seat.
Agent 02
Approval Routing
TriggerPricing request is submitted by rep or Agent 01.
InputsDeal size · margin delta · customer tier · rep track record.
ReasoningAuto-approve within guardrails or escalate to right authority with full context.
OutputInstant approval, or one-click escalation to manager / finance.
IntegrationsEmail · Slack · CRM workflow.
Human-in-loop Manager approves escalated deals with one-click; small deals route automatically.
Agent 03
Win / Loss Feedback
TriggerDeal is closed or marked lost in CRM.
InputsFinal price · competitor named · outcome · customer segment.
ReasoningUpdate pricing model, surface patterns (e.g., losing on SKU X in Region Y).
OutputWeekly insight digest to sales leadership; model retraining payload.
IntegrationsCRM · BI dashboard · pricing model.
Human-in-loop Quarterly pricing committee review; humans set strategy, agent surfaces signal.

Sysco · Foodservice Distribution
Current State Workflow
02

The Substitution Crisis

SKU Out-of-Stock → Order Fulfillment
01 / ORDER
Customer order lands in OMS
Restaurant, hospital, or institutional buyer.
Late discovery 02 / OOS HIT
Picker finds SKU is out at warehouse
Discovered hours before truck departs. Too late.
Manual call 03 / RING UP
CSR phones the customer, line by line
Each call ~8 minutes. Doesn't scale at peak.
Blind decision 04 / SUB OFFER
Rep offers a sub with little intelligence
No customer history, compliance fit, or margin lens.
Order shrinkage 05 / DECIDE
Customer accepts, declines, or partial-ships
Often frustrated. Avg ~12% line-item value lost.
06 / DELIVER
Truck rolls — partial or delayed
Invoice disputes follow. Relationship erodes.
Directional Impact
At ~2M order lines/week and a 3% OOS rate, Sysco handles 60K sub events weekly — ~8K rep-hours plus avg 12% order shrinkage per event.
Per-call Cost ~8 min
Order Shrink ~12%
Resolution Reactive

Slide 04 / 07
Sysco · Coordinated Agent Layer
System Design
02.2

Shift the decision earlier.

Catch OOS at the order desk, not the loading dock → proactive, not reactive
Agent 01
Substitution Intelligence
TriggerOOS flag fires in WMS at order capture or replenishment cycle.
InputsCustomer purchase history · compliance / dietary rules · contract pricing · margin · live inventory.
ReasoningRank top 3 subs by price parity, customer pattern, compliance fit, margin.
OutputRanked sub list with rationale, passed to Agent 02.
IntegrationsWMS · OMS · contract pricing DB.
Human-in-loop None at ranking — algorithmic, transparent, auditable.
Agent 02
Proactive Customer Outreach
Trigger24–48 hrs before delivery — or instantly for same-day orders.
InputsSub rankings · customer contact prefs · order details.
ReasoningPick best channel (SMS / portal / email), format options + price impact.
OutputSelf-serve outreach with one-tap selection link.
IntegrationsCustomer portal · SMS / email · CRM.
Human-in-loop CSR engaged only on customer escalation or non-response timeout.
Agent 03
Order Adjustment
TriggerCustomer selects sub — or response timeout reached.
InputsCustomer choice · current inventory · pricing confirmation.
ReasoningValidate stock, lock pricing, regenerate pick list and driver manifest.
OutputAuto-update OMS / WMS, push notice to driver app, confirm to customer.
IntegrationsWMS · OMS · driver mobile app.
Human-in-loop Exception-only: compliance flags, high-value account rejections.

Slide 06 / 07
90-Day Paid Pilot Plan
Scope · Metrics · Risk
04

Three phases. One renewal.

Foundation → Prove It → Expand · success = renewal conversation by day 75
Days 01 — 30
Foundation.
In Scope
  • 3M Pricing Intelligence Agent only
  • 1 sales region · 50 pricing requests as test set
  • CRM + email integration · read-only
Data Required
  • 12 mo pricing history
  • Margin floors · customer tiers
  • Win/loss data
Out of Scope
  • ERP write access
  • National rollout
  • Sysco integration
Days 31 — 60
Prove it.
In Scope
  • Add Approval Routing Agent
  • Expand to 3 regions
  • Begin Sysco data audit in parallel
Leading Metrics
  • Approval cycle time
  • Recommendation acceptance rate
  • Rep NPS on tool
Lagging Metrics
  • Margin on approved deals
  • Deals closed vs. prior period
Days 61 — 90
Expand.
In Scope
  • 3M national rollout
  • Sysco Sub Intel pilot · 1 DC, 1 segment
  • Renewal & expansion conversation by Day 75
Lagging Metrics
  • OOS resolution rate
  • Partial delivery rate
  • Order value retention on subs
Phase 2 Tee-Up
  • 3M Win/Loss Feedback Agent
  • Sysco Outreach + Order Adjustment
Risks & Mitigations
Data quality
Pricing history may be incomplete → rep validation week 1, exception flagging.
Adoption
Reps may bypass the tool → champion-rep program + manager visibility dashboard.
Scope creep
Sponsor will ask for more → hold the line. Everything new is "Phase 2."
